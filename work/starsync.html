Looking back

I grew up being interested in K-pop, so I designed StarSync from inside this community: K-pop and K-R&B fans at CMD. Talking to fellow interested ones, I realised a plain chat app wouldn’t be enough; it needed something that brings people together in real life. That became an AR challenge where you collect Hello Kitty figures in the Media Lounge to earn Stars.

The hardest part was making it interesting for people outside the community too. In a short project I couldn’t build everything I imagined, like dance challenges, idol trivia and merch on campus.
