Gisou Retail In-store concept

Context
Sephora Beauty Hub & EDG environments

“Winter Honey Rescue” is a retail experience concept for Gisou’s Sephora Beauty Hub and EDG environments, centred on the Honey Gloss Ceramide Therapy Hair Mask as the hero product.

The concept explores how campaign storytelling, product merchandising and spatial branding can create a cohesive luxury beauty experience in-store. The visual direction is warm light in cold weather: Gisou’s signature honey tones and soft creams, with a subtle winter blue as a seasonal cue for hydration.

For the Beauty Hub, the focus is service and education. A lifestyle panel tells the product story in short paragraphs aligned with the French Sephora guidelines, the tester tray guides customers through a three-step routine (cleanse, repair, seal), and the service menu repeats that routine with a clear call to action: “Essayez le rituel”.

For the EDG shelf, I designed a “try first, buy next” flow: testers on the top shelf with a “Try me” call-out, the mask and hair oil as the “repair + seal” pairing in the middle, and the wider Gisou universe below. The background visual explains why the mask works: ceramides, hyaluronic acid and Mirsalehi Honey.

All campaign artwork, layouts and retail execution decisions are my own; AI-generated product mockups were used only to visualise the concept in context.

Concept

Warm light in cold weather: a cocooning, glowing moment inside while it is winter outside.

Artwork

Every surface designed to Sephora’s specifications, from service menu to shelf covers.

In store

Two environments, one ritual: cleanse, repair, seal.

Looking back

Moving from screens to shelves asked for a different way of thinking. Designing to Sephora’s exact specifications, from tray sizes to shelf covers and French copy, felt restrictive at first, but those limits made every decision more deliberate.

What I like most is that one simple routine, cleanse, repair, seal, could guide both a service counter and a shelf. I was also open about using AI mock-ups only to show the concept in context. The artwork and layouts are mine.
