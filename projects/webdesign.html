GISOU Digital Design Concept

Role
UX/UI Design, Visual Design, Responsive Design, CRM
Tools
Figma

"The Icon of Honey Glow" is a premium e-commerce campaign concept created for Gisou, centered around the Honey Infused Hair Oil as the brand's iconic hero product.

The project explored how editorial storytelling, luxury beauty aesthetics, and conversion-focused digital design could be combined into a cohesive cross-channel campaign experience across desktop, mobile, and CRM touchpoints.

The homepage was structured as a narrative journey, moving from emotional campaign storytelling toward product discovery, ritual-based merchandising, and community-focused conversion moments. Inspired by Gisou's honey-infused identity and Mirsalehi heritage, the visual direction focused on warm neutral tones, spacious layouts, strong hierarchy, and premium editorial imagery.

Alongside the responsive landing page, a complementary CRM email concept was developed to extend the campaign into a realistic luxury beauty e-commerce ecosystem.

This project allowed me to explore the intersection between branding, responsive UX/UI design, digital merchandising, and campaign storytelling within a commercial e-commerce context.
